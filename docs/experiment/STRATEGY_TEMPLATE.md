## Learnings

{This section is updated after each trading day when new learnings are added}

### 2026-02-15

**Observation**: Bought TSLA at $220 based on positive earnings report

**Outcome**: Stock dropped 8% over next 3 days despite good earnings due to broader tech selloff

**Lesson**: Don't buy immediately after positive news; wait 1-2 days for market reaction to stabilize. Also check broader sector trends before entering positions.

---

### 2026-02-14

**Observation**: Held AAPL through earnings announcement

**Outcome**: Stock jumped 12% on earnings beat, realized $85 gain when sold next day

**Lesson**: Holding through earnings can work for high-conviction positions with strong fundamentals, but only if prepared to hold through potential drop.

---

### 2026-02-12

**Observation**: Sold NVDA position at 5% profit to avoid holding through weekend

**Outcome**: Stock rallied 15% on Monday; missed $150 in gains

**Lesson**: Weekend risk aversion can be costly. Consider holding high-conviction positions through weekends unless there are specific risks (pending news, geopolitical events).

---

### 2026-02-10

**Observation**: Diversified into 5 different stocks with small positions

**Outcome**: Transaction taxes on multiple small trades ate into returns ($105 in taxes on $500 in trades)

**Lesson**: Position sizes should be meaningful (at least $150+) to offset 35% tax drag. Better to concentrate in fewer high-conviction trades than spread too thin. Tax burden is severe for active trading.

---

### 2026-02-08

**Observation**: Sold half of winning position (MSFT +25%) to lock in profits

**Outcome**: Remaining half continued to climb (+15% more over next week)

**Lesson**: Taking some profits is good risk management, but consider letting winners run if fundamentals remain strong. Could use trailing stops instead of fixed profit targets.
